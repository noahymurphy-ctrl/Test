Our Process

Nothing is rushed.

01 The Dough

Stone-milled flour,
cold butter, time.

We use a single-origin heritage wheat, milled to order. The dough rests for eighteen hours before a single fold is made.

02 The Lamination

Twelve folds.
Two days.

Each block of dough is hand-laminated twelve times using 84% fat European butter. No shortcuts. No sheeter. Only a rolling pin and restraint.

03 The Proof

Slow cold proof.
Baked to order.

Shaped rolls proof overnight in a cold retarder, then bake directly from cold into a preheated deck oven. The layers do the rest.
