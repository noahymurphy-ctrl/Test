Our Process

Made with intention.

01 The Dough

Stone-milled. Cold-fermented.

We source heritage wheat from a single mill in Vermont. The dough cold-ferments for eighteen hours before it is ever touched — developing the deep, complex flavor that shortcuts cannot replicate.

02 The Lamination

Twelve folds. Two days.

European-style cultured butter is locked into the dough and folded twelve times by hand. Each fold is followed by a rest. The result: impossibly thin layers that shatter on first bite.

03 The Proof

Overnight. Unhurried.

Shaped rolls proof through the night in a cold retard. They enter the oven at dawn, still cold — the abrupt heat shock creating the open, layered crumb that defines ROULÉ.
